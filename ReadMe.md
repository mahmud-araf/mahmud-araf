## 💫 About Me

Hi, I'm **Abdullah Al Mahmud**, you can call me **Araf** — a **BSc CSE** graduate from the **University of Dhaka**, software engineer, and serial hackathon winner from Bangladesh.

I build **production-grade full-stack systems** with a strong focus on **AI/ML integration**, clean architecture, and real-world impact. My work spans web platforms, mobile apps, Shopify apps, and agentic AI systems.

- 🏆 **4× National Hackathon Champion** across Bangladesh's most prestigious competitions
- 💼 Currently working as **Software Engineer** at [One Degree Labs](https://joinodl.com) (LA, USA) & [Mass Holding Co.](https://www.mass-hd.com) (Tokyo, Japan) — simultaneously
- 🚀 Co-Founder & Lead Engineer at **Donoro** (blood donation platform, live on Google Play)
